Questions & Knowledge Base

Answers before you onboard.

Can I run my existing DAT account in ZONIX with full admin control?

Yes, 100%. You maintain full administrative authority over your DAT account. You can invite operators, provision dedicated seats, and monitor activity in real time. Because ZONIX isolates hardware fingerprints (WebGL, Canvas, Audio, Screen resolution) and binds sessions to clean, sticky residential proxies, DAT sees only a single stable, legitimate machine. You get zero account bans, zero multi-IP blocks, and complete administrative control without ever sharing your master passwords.

Can I buy a DAT account directly from ZONIX?

Yes! For dispatch fleets, independent dispatchers, or expanding freight agencies needing immediate capacity without setup delays, we offer pre-verified, turnkey DAT One access provisioned directly into your ZONIX client. Contact our 24/7 Dispatch Desk via WhatsApp or our Instant Access Desk to get set up in minutes.

Does ZONIX prevent DAT One and Truckstop account bans?

Yes. Loadboard algorithms trigger flags when multiple dispatchers log in simultaneously with different IP addresses, conflicting timezones, or changing canvas fingerprints. ZONIX routes all dispatchers through consistent sticky proxy nodes and mirrors the master admin's hardware profile exactly, presenting a single stable identity.

Do my dispatchers see my passwords or loadboard logins?

No. Passwords never touch the dispatcher's computer. The admin signs into the session vault once. The dispatcher client only receives sealed authorization tokens. If an operator leaves your organization, you can terminate their session in one click from the admin portal.

What happens if a proxy node drops during active dispatch?

ZONIX's zero-leak killswitch engages in 0.05ms. It immediately freezes the viewport and blocks all outbound socket traffic, preventing the dispatcher's real home IP address from leaking to DAT. Once the backup proxy connects, the session resumes seamlessly.

How fast can our dispatch fleet get set up?

Under 5 minutes. Download the desktop installer (`v1.9.31`), create your organization, invite your dispatchers via email or invite link, and mount your master session.
