How it works

One admin login. Entire team, in sync.

ZONIX splits your workflow into two secure roles — Admin captures the session once, Dispatchers inherit authenticated access instantly, with zero visibility into raw credentials.

01
Login once

Admin authenticates DAT, Truckstop, 123 Loadboard and carrier portals inside the secure ZONIX shell.

02
Capture cookies & storage

Session cookies, localStorage tokens and CSRF handles are captured in a sealed vault.

03
Encrypt & sign

Payload is AES-256 encrypted and cryptographically signed before leaving the machine.

04
Publish to registry

Sealed session hits the ZONIX registry — dispatchers get instant access, credentials never do.

FAQ

Answers, before you ask.

How does cookie sync work?

The admin logs in through the ZONIX shell, which captures cookies, localStorage, and CSRF tokens into an AES-256 sealed vault. Dispatchers mount that vault into an isolated tab — no raw credentials ever touch their machine.

Is my password safe?

Yes. Passwords never leave the admin device. Only signed session artifacts are transported, and they can be revoked from the registry with one click.

Do I have to open the admin browser daily?

No. ZONIX auto-refreshes eligible sessions in the background and alerts you only when a platform requires a re-auth (e.g. after a password change).

Does it support DAT?

Yes — DAT One and DAT Power are first-class. Discord, Zoho, Truckstop, Carrier411 and most broker portals are supported out of the box.

Will dispatchers get flagged for multi-login?

The anti-detect engine mirrors the admin's fingerprint and pins each session to a consistent proxy. Platforms see one stable device, not a team of dispatchers.
